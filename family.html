This Short Term Rental Agreement ("Agreement") is made by and between Sugarcrest, LLLP ("Sugarcrest") and Guest as of the date of Guest's booking.

Rental Party & Maximum Occupancy

Up to 16 adults. Additional charge of $100 per person per night for guests exceeding 16.

Rental Term

Check-in: 4:00 PM Central Time. Check-out: 11:00 AM Central Time.

Cancellation Policy

Smoking is NOT allowed inside. Guest agrees to pay not less than $450 for odor abatement if smoking occurs inside.

Guest and rental Party assume the risk of injury related to weather, falling trees, and any activities on the property including all lake/swimming/dock/beach areas.

Maximum two non-aggressive breed pets permitted on a case-by-case, Owner-approved basis.

Water and Septic

The cabins are on well and septic systems. DO NOT FLUSH anything other than toilet paper.

Aquatic Invasive Species (AIS)

Under Minnesota law, watercraft must be properly decontaminated if coming from an AIS-affected water.

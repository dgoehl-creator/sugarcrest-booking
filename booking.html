SHORT TERM RENTAL AGREEMENT
This Agreement is made between Sugarcrest, LLLP ("Sugarcrest") and Guest as of the date of booking.

Property

37857 North Sugar Lake Road, Cohasset, MN 55721. Two furnished cabins including linens, towels, and kitchen serving ware.

Maximum Occupancy

Maximum 16 overnight guests. Additional guests over 16: $100/person/night.

Check-in / Check-out

Check-in: 4:00 PM Central. Check-out: 11:00 AM Central.

Cancellation Policy

100% refund if canceled 120+ days prior · 75% if 90–119 days · 50% if 60–89 days · No refund if 59 or fewer days prior. Trip insurance recommended: insuremytrip.com.

All property must be left undamaged. Sugarcrest reserves the right to charge Guest for damage beyond normal wear and tear.

Pets

Maximum two non-aggressive breed pets with Owner approval only. Keep leashed, clean up after, wipe down before entering cabins.

Water & Septic

DO NOT FLUSH anything other than toilet paper. No feminine products. Violations may result in significant additional fees.

Fire Pit & Fireplace

Outdoor fires in the designated fire pit only. Open fireplace damper before use, close after. Never leave fires unattended. Fully extinguish before retiring.

Golf Course

Use at own risk. No children under 12 without adult supervision.

Owner's Property

Garages and Sissieville storage shed are off-limits.

Aquatic Invasive Species (AIS)

Minnesota law prohibits transporting aquatic plants or invasive species. Watercraft must be decontaminated if coming from an AIS-affected water. See MN DNR.

Rental Party Use Only

No visitors or additional overnight guests without Owner's prior written permission.
